## Overview

This project seeks to develop a psychological conceptualization of religious pluralism by synthesizing findings across psychology, sociology, education, philosophy, and religious studies.

The project identifies how religious pluralism has been defined, operationalized, and measured, with the goal of proposing a framework that is theoretically grounded in psychological science.
