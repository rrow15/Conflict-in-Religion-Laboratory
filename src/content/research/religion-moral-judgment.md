## Overview

This project investigates how people evaluate moral transgressions when the person committing the transgression belongs to either their own or another social group.

The study examines the roles of moral emotions, group identity, and religious beliefs in shaping judgments of wrongdoing and perceptions of those who violate moral expectations.
