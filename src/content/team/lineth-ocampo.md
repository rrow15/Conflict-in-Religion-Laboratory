## Bio Note

Lineth is a registered Psychometrician and a dedicated mental health advocate with a strong interest in promoting the safety, well-being, and empowerment of children and women. She earned her Bachelor’s degree in Psychology from Centro Escolar University Manila and is currently pursuing her Master’s degree in Clinical Psychology at De La Salle University. Lineth's research interests generally revolve around mental health and lived experiences of vulnerable groups. She has gained experience in psychological assessment, research, and mental health support through various academic, professional, and volunteer engagements. Outside of work and academics, Lineth enjoys volunteering, learning, and spending time with her dogs.
