## Bio Note

Constanza is a Chilean-registered psychologist and a Master's student in Clinical Psychology at De La Salle University, interested in the clinical psychology of religion. She completed her undergraduate training in Chile with highest distinction and holds specialized certification in psychodynamic diagnosis (OPD-2). Her clinical background spans close to a decade across social protection, public healthcare, and private practice. Since 2018 she has maintained a bilingual (English/Spanish) practice serving globally-mobile, multicultural, and religiously diverse adults. Her research examines how religious/spiritual struggles and representations of the divine affect mental health under stress. She is drawn to the spaces where different worldviews meet, and hopes to serve people on the difficult edges of cross-cultural and religious life.
