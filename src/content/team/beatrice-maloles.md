## Bio Note

Patricia Beatrice Maloles, known as Bea, is a graduate student at De La Salle University, Manila, taking up her Master of Arts in Psychology, major in Human Development. Her first ever journal article, entitled Conversions from High-Cost Religious Groups: Narratives From a Non-WEIRD Family-Centered Culture, was published in August 2026. Bea graduated Magna Cum Laude from the University of the Philippines, Diliman, with an undergraduate degree of Bachelor of Arts in Anthropology. Despite coming from a different discipline during her undergraduate days, Bea aims to use her knowledge of Anthropology and culture to understand the holistic development of individuals, especially applying this viewpoint to the study of Psychology in the Philippines. She has an interest in Positive Youth Development and wishes to apply and promote it here in the Philippines. She hopes to become a researcher and professor one day, using all that she has learned to empower future generations.
