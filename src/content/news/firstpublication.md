The Conflict in Religion Psychology Research (CORE) Laboratory is thrilled to share the release of its first publication under the guidance of our lab! Published in Pastoral Psychology, this study entitled Conversions from High-Cost Religious Groups: Narratives From a Non-WEIRD Family-Centered Culture offers fresh, compelling insights into the dynamics of faith and identity.

We invite our fellow scholars, students, and community members to engage with this exciting work! Access the full paper for free here: 👉 https://rdcu.be/fz20u
