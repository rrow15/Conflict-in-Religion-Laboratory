In the third day of the PAP Convention, several members of the CORE Lab, Spirit Lab, as well as the ADMU Flipp Lab presented their respective symposia. Convened by Dr. Darren Dumaop, the topics centered on "Religious Identity, Conversion, and Pluralism in a Changing Society". The event ensued from 9:30 AM to 11:00 AM at the  Ateneo de Davao University Senior High campus.
