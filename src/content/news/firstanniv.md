This September marks the first anniversary of CORE (Conflict in Religion Psychology Research Group)! 🎉
Over the past year, our team of master’s students, undergraduates from inside and outside DLSU, and dedicated interns have worked together to pursue our mission: to study how religion can be a source of conflict in predominantly religious societies.
Despite being a young research group, we are proud to have produced three research outputs awaiting for publication. Moving forward, we are excited to deepen our engagement with research stakeholders to share the benefits of our work and create meaningful impact.
Here’s to more learning, growth, and collaboration as we begin our second year! 🙌
