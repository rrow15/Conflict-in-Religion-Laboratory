🎉 Celebrating a Major Milestone! 🎉

The Conflict in Religion Psychology Research (CORE) Laboratory is thrilled to share the release of its first publication under the guidance of our lab!

Published in Pastoral Psychology, this study entitled Conversions from High-Cost Religious Groups: Narratives From a Non-WEIRD Family-Centered Culture offers fresh, compelling insights into the dynamics of faith and identity.

🧠 Why This Research Matters

Traditional conversion models often place individual autonomy at the center, relying heavily on frameworks developed within Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, and Democratic (WEIRD) contexts.

Through the lived experiences of four converts, the paper offers an expanded, culturally attuned explanation of religious conversion. This work highlights how, in non-WEIRD and collectivist societies, relational bonds and family serve as powerful catalysts for transformation.

It is a vital step forward in diversifying psychological literature and honoring the nuanced, family-centered narratives of our local culture.
