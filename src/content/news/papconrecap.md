At the 62nd Annual Psychological Association of the Philippines Convention last weekend, members of the DLSU CORE Lab in collaboration with the ADMU FLIPP Lab, and in a concurrent session with the DLSU SPIRIT Lab, delivered symposia on their research breakthroughs in religion, spirituality, and social psychology, convened by their respective directors, Dr. Darren Dumaop, Dr. Erwine Dela Paz, and Dr. Homer Yabut.
Their sessions featured presentations of their recent studies and theory development and provided a space for knowledge exchange with fellow scholars and experts in the field.
We congratulate the emerging research labs in the psychology of religion for taking this opportunity and contributing to the advancement of the field in the Philippines!
