This Saturday, CORE director Dr. Darren and members convened for their first Journal Reading Club hybrid session. The members brought their brains together to discuss a construct with large research potential, religious harm. Spearheaded by Ms. Ma. Elena Severina Cruz, the team assessed the merit and sociocultural transportability of Hollier, Clifton, and Bobo's 2026 article, "Mapping the Impact of Religious Harm: Results from a Large-Scale Quantitative study. This paper, along with others delving into associated phenomena, is substantive and are expected to be good grounds for a future pilot study and scale validation projects.
