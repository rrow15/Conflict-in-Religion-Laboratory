This Saturday, CORE director Dr. Darren and members convened for their first Journal Reading Club session. Held in a hybrid setup, the members brought their brains together to discuss a construct with large research potential, religious harm. Spearheaded by Ms. Ma. Elena Severina Cruz, the team assessed the merit and transportability of Hollier, Clifton, and Bobo's 2026 article, "Mapping the Impact of Religious Harm: Results from a Large-Scale Quantitative study, in the Philippine context. This paper, along with others delving into associated phenomena such as spiritual harm, spiritual abuse, religious trauma syndrome, and others, are truly felt and are expected to serve as good grounds for a future pilot study and even scale validation projects.
