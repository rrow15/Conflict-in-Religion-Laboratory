Let us extend our warmest congratulations to our very own Dr. Darren Dumaop, Ms. Patricia Beatrice Maloles, Ms. Maria Elena Severina-Cruz, and Ms. Lindsey Ng-Tan, who received the prestigious Alfredo V. Lagmay Research Award at this year's Psychological Association of the Philippines Annual Convention! 🎉 

The paper sheds light on the important role of family and other significant relationships as catalysts for change in individuals' belief systems and social groups. 

We are very proud of your achievement and look forward to your future feats!
