Courtesy of the International Psychology Students Organization (IPSYSO), Dr. Darren Dumaop joined GPT-3 as a distinguished speaker.

📌 He presented on the topic: "Towards a WILD Psychology of Religion: An Introduction and Challenge".

The talk was open to all participants who were interested in engaging with diverse psychological perspectives in an international academic setting and was held via Zoom this Saturday at 11:00 AM.
